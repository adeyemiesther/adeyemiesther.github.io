What most stores are missing

Why most online stores struggle to sell today.

Traffic is more expensive than ever and shoppers are less patient. These are the gaps we find again and again when we audit stores, and how we fix them.

Slow mobile experience

Your store is slow on phones

Most shoppers browse on mobile. Heavy images, too many apps and bloated themes make pages lag, and visitors leave before they see a product.

The fixSpeed optimisation, image compression, app clean-up and a lighter theme setup.
No email & SMS flows

You lose buyers who don't check out

Most visitors leave without buying. Without welcome, abandoned-cart, browse-abandonment and post-purchase flows, that money never comes back.

The fixKlaviyo email & SMS flows that follow up automatically, 24/7.
Weak product pages

Product pages don't build desire

Thin descriptions, few photos, no video, no size guides and no answers to common questions leave shoppers unsure, and unsure shoppers don't buy.

The fixConversion copy, rich media, FAQs, comparison blocks and clear calls to action.
Low trust

Visitors don't trust you yet

No reviews, no customer photos, unclear shipping and returns, and no real brand story. New shoppers need proof before they hand over their card.

The fixReviews & UGC, trust badges, clear policies and an "About" that sells.
Invisible in search

Google and AI search can't find you

Missing titles, meta descriptions, alt text and structured data mean you don't show up on Google, Google Shopping, or the AI assistants shoppers now ask.

The fixTechnical & on-page SEO, product schema and content built for search.
Broken tracking

You don't know what's working

Without GA4, the Meta Pixel and Conversions API set up properly, ads optimise blindly and you can't tell which products, pages or campaigns make money.

The fixClean analytics, pixel & CAPI setup and a simple monthly dashboard.
Low order value

Customers buy one thing and leave

No bundles, upsells, free-shipping thresholds or "frequently bought together" means every order is smaller than it could be.

The fixBundles, cart upsells, tiered offers and smart free-shipping goals.
Checkout friction

Checkout is too much work

Surprise costs, forced account creation and no express payment options cause people to abandon right at the finish line.

The fixExpress pay (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, PayPal), clear costs and a smoother checkout.
No retention plan

Customers buy once and forget you

Chasing new buyers every month is expensive. Without loyalty, reviews requests, win-back emails or subscriptions, repeat sales stay low.

The fixLoyalty & referral programs, win-back flows and subscriptions.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What platforms do you work with?

Mainly Shopify, plus WooCommerce, WordPress, Wix and Etsy. We also work with tools like Klaviyo, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Zapier, Make and GoHighLevel.

Is the store audit really free?

Yes. The scan runs instantly on this page. To unlock the full list of issues and download the PDF report, enter your name, email and WhatsApp number, then send us the short WhatsApp message the page prepares for you.

How much will my project cost?

Small fixes start from $50, store improvements from $150 and custom builds from $500. Your final price is confirmed in writing after we understand the scope.

How long does a project take?

Small fixes can take a few days. Store improvements usually take 1–2 weeks and full builds 2–4 weeks, depending on scope and how quickly content is ready.

Do you guarantee sales or Google rankings?

No honest agency can. We focus on the things that are proven to help: speed, trust, clear product pages, follow-up emails and good SEO, and we're clear about what can and can't be promised.

Can you work on my existing store?

Yes. Most of our work is improving existing stores. We start with an audit, then agree what to change.

How do payments work?

Price, deliverables and timeline are agreed in writing before work starts. Payment terms are confirmed in your quote.
